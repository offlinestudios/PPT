# Requirements and government-link audit — 21 September 2026

Changes are local and unpublished. Original generated pages had explicit government requirement links for only four document entries; most others linked back to their own service page. The previous review prototype added a few sources but recovered old digital rules without fully verifying them. That is now replaced by an application-specific dataset.

## Corrections

- Canadian passport online renewal now includes JPEG/JPG, 1200 × 1800–3000 × 4500 pixels, 200 KB–5 MB, 45–50% face height, original camera file and photographer details. A scanned print is a display sample only.
- PR-card uploads: 715 × 1000–2000 × 2800 pixels and up to 4 MB; not the passport rules.
- U.S. passport renewal and U.S. visa limits are separate.
- Irish, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Hong Kong and Chinese passport instructions are application-specific. Polish online child-passport dimensions are not the Polish ID-card dimensions.
- OCI current portal says 200–900 pixels square and up to 200 KB; older 1500-pixel values are not reused.
- France and Germany have one grey-background service statement; digital-file availability does not imply every consular route accepts uploads.
- Scanned studio sample used across passport selections and labelled as a print sample, not country-specific proof of compliance.

## Limits of this audit

Numeric digital guidance is shown only where supported by reviewed official material. Some sources expose specifications only inside an application portal. Source-only rows do not establish a verified numeric file limit. USCIS blocked automated retrieval; Iran's endpoint could not be read; Brazil's Toronto page presented a challenge; Vietnam's current instructions could not be fetched. No attempts were made to bypass access controls. School IDs, broad service hubs and unspecified licence/student-visa pages have no single government source. Most inherited print details have not been comprehensively re-audited; only the explicitly corrected print records should be considered checked in this pass.
