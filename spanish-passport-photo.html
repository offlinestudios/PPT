Spanish Passport photo requirements

Printed photo

  • Size: 32mm × 26mm
  • Plain white background
  • Full face view directly facing camera
  • Neutral natural expression
  • Eyes open and clearly visible
  • Head uncovered unless permitted for religious/medical reasons
  • Even lighting with no shadows
  • Recent colour photo

Digital photo

  • The consular checklist asks for a printed photo. Confirm with the consulate before substituting a digital file.
